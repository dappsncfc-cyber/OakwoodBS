About Oakwood Building Services Norfolk Ltd

Reliable Builders & Craftsmanship Across North Norfolk

Oakwood Building Services Norfolk Ltd delivers high-quality residential construction, property renovations, and bespoke building work across North Norfolk—serving homeowners in Cromer, Sheringham, Holt, Aylsham, North Walsham, and surrounding villages.

From complex multi-storey house extensions and sympathetic barn conversions to modern kitchen installations, structural alterations, and traditional brick and flint repointing, we provide reliable workmanship backed by clear communication and meticulous site management.

Our reputation is built on honest pricing, skilled local tradespeople, and complete respect for your home throughout every phase of construction. From initial groundwork to final finishing details, we manage your project with professionalism and care.
