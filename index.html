About Oakwood Building Services Norfolk Ltd

Reliable Builders & Craftsmanship Across North Norfolk

Oakwood Building Services Norfolk Ltd delivers high-quality residential construction, property renovations, and bespoke building work across North Norfolk—serving homeowners in Cromer, Sheringham, Holt, Aylsham, North Walsham, Stalham, and the surrounding coastal and rural villages.

From complex multi-storey house extensions and sympathetic barn conversions to modern kitchen installations, structural alterations, and traditional brick and flint repointing, we provide a reliable, fully project-managed building service tailored to your property.

Our reputation is built on clear, honest communication, skilled local tradespeople, and complete respect for your home throughout every phase of construction. From initial groundwork to final sign-off, every detail matters to us—because every completed project reflects the Oakwood name.
